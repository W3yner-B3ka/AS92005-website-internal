Mars

Mars is the fourth planet of the solar system with a similar size and surface to Earth. Its diameter is around 6,779km.

Gravity

3.71 m/s²

38% of Earth's gravity

Atmosphere

95% CO₂

Thin, unbreathable air

Day Length

24.6 hrs

Close to an Earth day

01

Red
Dust

The surface of Mars is full of iron oxide — fine rust particles that scatter sunlight. This results in the red atmosphere.

500M km² of rust-red terrain
02

Mountains

Olympus Mons is the tallest volcano in the solar system. Its base is so wide it could cover France entirely. Its peak pierces the upper atmosphere at 21 kilometres high.

21km above the Martian surface
03

Valleys

Valles Marineris stretches 4,000km across the Martian surface — long enough to span the entire United States. A canyon so deep it could swallow the Grand Canyon ten times over.

7km deep at its lowest point
